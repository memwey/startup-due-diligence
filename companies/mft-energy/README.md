## TL;DR

MFT Energy (MFT Energy A/S, CVR 38175130) is an energy trading company headquartered in Aarhus, Denmark. The legal entity was registered in November 2016, and the company states that it began trading in April 2017 and gives 2017 as its founding year. It calls itself an independent trading hub for power, gas and "environmentals" (energy certificates), trading 24/7 on exchanges and bilaterally across intraday, day-ahead and forward markets, with offices in Denmark, Türkiye, Singapore, Australia, the US and Japan. Since January 2026 it has held 68% of Northium Energy ApS, a Danish developer of battery energy storage systems (BESS). In Japan it operates through MFT Energy APAC3株式会社, which is listed as a JEPX trading member and as an OCCTO member in the retail electricity business (小売電気事業者) category.

- FY2025 net trading income (gross profit) was EUR 37.7m, EBIT EUR 2.8m and profit EUR 1.9m. The FY2024 figures were EUR 80.1m, EUR 46.4m and EUR 36.8m, and the peak year was FY2022, with EBIT of EUR 576.2m. FY2026 guidance is EBIT of EUR 10m–50m ([Annual Report 2025](https://mft-energy.com/wp-content/uploads/MFT-Annual-Report-2025.pdf), pp. 7, 31; signed 2026-04-21).
- The company had 193 employees at 2025-12-31 (185 average FTE), and 69 of them were partners (co-owners). The workforce covered 29 nationalities ([Annual Report 2025](https://mft-energy.com/wp-content/uploads/MFT-Annual-Report-2025.pdf), pp. 7, 9, 24).
- No external venture or private-equity round was found. The group is controlled by MFT Energy Holding ApS ([Annual Report 2025](https://mft-energy.com/wp-content/uploads/MFT-Annual-Report-2025.pdf), note 27). The CVR extract lists seven holding companies as that entity's legal owners with 5% or more ([CVR roles extract via eStatistik](https://estatistik.dk/virksomhed/mft-energy-holding-aps/38174207/roller); accessed 2026-10-11).
- The trading systems are built in-house. Automated and algorithmic trading executed more than 60% of short-term power trades in 2024 ([Annual Report 2024](https://mft-energy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/MFT-2024-Annual-report.pdf), p. 24). Job postings describe C#/.NET and Python on Microsoft Azure ([2022 posting](https://web.archive.org/web/20220703054731/https://career.mft-energy.com/jobs/1686404-net-software-developer), [2026 posting](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4458247030)). The corporate language is English ([graduate page FAQ](https://mft-energy.com/graduate/); Undated; accessed 2026-10-11).

### Market context as stated by the company

From the [Annual Report 2025](https://mft-energy.com/wp-content/uploads/MFT-Annual-Report-2025.pdf) (pp. 12, 21, 29):

- It states that LNG became the dominant source of European gas supply in 2025, at about 40%. It also states that EU gas storage was 62% full at year-end, against 72% a year earlier.

### Trading scope
