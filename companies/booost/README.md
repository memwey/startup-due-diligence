## TL;DR

Booost (Booost株式会社 / Booost, Inc.; corporate number 7010001167327) is a Tokyo-based company founded on 2015-04-15. It sells **booost Sustainability**, which it calls a "サステナビリティERP" (sustainability ERP): a SaaS platform for collecting, approving, calculating and disclosing ESG and greenhouse-gas data across corporate groups and supply chains, aimed mainly at large Japanese listed companies preparing for SSBJ disclosure. The same legal entity was registered as 株式会社日本新電力総合研究所, renamed booost technologies株式会社 on 2020-04-01 and Booost株式会社 on 2025-04-02 ([NTA corporate number register](https://www.houjin-bangou.nta.go.jp/henkorireki-johoto.html?selHouzinNo=7010001167327); accessed 2026-10-11). The business started in electricity energy management and still lists a retail electricity registration ([company profile](https://booost.inc/COMPANY_about); accessed 2026-10-11).

- Company-stated adoption: 95+ countries, about 6,500 companies (cumulative) and 197,000+ sites as of December 2025 ([2026-01-14](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000237.000056793.html)). In September 2025 the same metric read about 2,000 companies ([2025-09-29](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000211.000056793.html)) — see `Notes`.
- Latest round: **シリーズC 1stクローズ, 7億円**, from One Capital (lead) and プロネクサス, scheduled to complete 2026-03-31; the company says this brings funding since the Series A to **41.5億円** ([2026-03-26](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000254.000056793.html)). Earlier company figures for the same cumulative total do not add up — see `Notes`.
- Headcount: 100 in 2025 on the company's own chart, up from 55 in 2021 ([company introduction deck](https://speakerdeck.com/booost/hui-she-shao-jie-zi-liao-booostzhu-shi-hui-she); accessed 2026-10-11). As of November 2025, 47% of employees (excluding officers) were in product roles and 10% were foreign nationals (same deck).
- Engineering: PHP/Laravel (partly Python) backend, React.js / Vue.js / TypeScript frontend, MySQL, on AWS, per current job postings ([HRMOS](https://hrmos.co/pages/booost/jobs/991960580122120278087); accessed 2026-10-11); the company's Zenn articles describe a Laravel monolith with a new React SPA ([2025-09-18](https://zenn.dev/booost/articles/114ffed27ad29e)). Engineering postings require Japanese; hybrid work with one office day a week (Wednesday) in Tokyo or Osaka.

### Market context as stated by the company

- SSBJ standards apply from the fiscal year ending March 2027 to TSE Prime companies with market capitalization of ¥3 trillion or more (about 70 companies), then ¥1 trillion or more (about 200), then ¥500 billion or more (about 300), with eventual application expected for all ~1,600 Prime companies ([2026-04-15](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000258.000056793.html)).
- The company coined "サステナビリティ2026問題" for what it describes as companies being late and insufficiently alarmed in preparing for mandatory disclosure, and launched a 「日本をSX先進国へ」 project around it in November 2024 ([2024-11-28](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000146.000056793.html)).
- In a company survey, 12.7% of companies said their SSBJ work was led by the CFO line, while sustainability departments led in 30.9%, the most common answer ([2026-08-05](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000280.000056793.html)).

## Product

**booost Sustainability** is presented as an "統合型SXプラットフォーム" handling "1,200以上のデータポイント" across environment, social and governance, with multi-level approval flows across group companies and supply chains and design for third-party assurance ([2026-03-26](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000254.000056793.html)). Customers combine modules ([deck, slide 37](https://speakerdeck.com/booost/hui-she-shao-jie-zi-liao-booostzhu-shi-hui-she)). Product pages are on [booost-ai.com](https://booost-ai.com/).
