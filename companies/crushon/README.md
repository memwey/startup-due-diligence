## TL;DR

CrushOn AI is an adults-only character-chat service at `crushon.ai`, titled "CrushOn AI - No Filter NSFW Character AI Chat - Spicy AI GF", where users create AI characters and chat with them under a monthly message-credit subscription ([homepage](https://crushon.ai/); accessed 2026-08-14). The service names four different operating entities across its own documents in under three years: no entity at all through early 2025, then `Crushon AI Corp.` in Bellevue, Washington, then `CRUSHON AI INC.` in Vancouver, British Columbia, and since mid-2025 `TECHIEPIE LTD`, a Cyprus company registered 2025-03-19 ([archived footers](https://web.archive.org/web/20250306123450/https://crushon.ai/terms-of-service), [Terms of Use](https://crushon.ai/terms-of-service); Last modified 2025-07-22) — see `Identity and legal entities`.

- The Terms state "The Service is provided by TECHIEPIE LTD, a company registered in Cyprus", while the governing-law clause says "the Service shall be deemed solely based in Hong Kong" and is "governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the HK"; the footer additionally carries a Delaware address ([Terms of Use](https://crushon.ai/terms-of-service); Last modified 2025-07-22).
- Pricing is a seven-tier message-credit subscription: free at 100 credits/month, then US$5.99 to US$199.99 per month (US$58.88 to US$1,799.99 per year) for 2,000 to 125,000 credits, plus a "Pro Models" chat package add-on ([pricing page](https://crushon.ai/pricing); read in a browser 2026-08-14).
- The company publishes no user, revenue or character-count figure anywhere on the site. The independently observable numbers are the official Discord — 71,491 members, 6,755 online ([Discord invite API](https://discord.com/api/v10/invites/crushonai?with_counts=true); observed 2026-08-14) — and Similarweb's public profile, which reported 22.4M visits and a global rank of #1,512 for the July 2026 reference period ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/crushon.ai/)).
- No funding announcement exists. [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/crushon-ai/__Q35HFbL4EOKKbeaPG1IMIImXSmSm5PMMERN6lpfOev8) states "Crushon AI has not raised any funding yet", while [StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/investment_rounds/crushonai-funding-round-2026) asserts a US$15M round on 2026-04-29 with no cited source — see `Funding`.
- Engineering evidence comes entirely from the served application: Next.js App Router behind Cloudflare bot protection, with self-hosted Sentry, self-hosted GrowthBook feature flags and a self-hosted SensorsData analytics endpoint; the privacy policy states "We may use User Content to train AI models" and names no model provider (page resources inspected 2026-08-14).

## Product

### What it is

CrushOn AI is a character-chat platform: users create characters, chat with characters made by themselves or other users, and pay for message capacity. The navigation exposes Home, Recent Chats, Create, Rankings, Profile, Pricing, Store, Bonus and Notifications ([homepage](https://crushon.ai/); accessed 2026-08-14). The Terms describe it as "a generative artificial intelligence (AI) chat functionality" where "The basic Service is free to use for customers and message credits can be purchased for full use" ([Terms of Use](https://crushon.ai/terms-of-service)).

### Content rules as published

The Community Guidelines set rules that sit alongside the "No Filter NSFW" positioning in the page title ([Community Guidelines](https://crushon.ai/community-guidelines); accessed via [archive, 2026-07-16](https://web.archive.org/web/20260716045138/https://crushon.ai/community-guidelines)):

- **Age:** "Crushon is available only to users 18 and older."
- **Minors in content:** "Do not create or depict characters who are minors or whose age is not clearly defined as over 18. This includes 'aged-up' characters, originally depicted as minors in source material. Realistic or human-like depictions of underage characters, even if fictional, are strictly prohibited."
- **Real people:** "Do not post real images or overly realistic AI-generated images of any individuals … to protect individual privacy and prevent the misuse of someone's likeness without consent"; impersonation of real individuals without consent is listed as removable.
- **Sexual imagery:** "Avoid depicting explicit sexual activity and stimulation. Implied sexual activity is permitted when contextually appropriate (medical, educational, fictional representation)."
- **Illegal activity:** "Do not use the Crushon Service to engage in or promote illegal activities, including commercial sexual activity, trafficking, or pornography."
- **Other prohibitions:** hate speech including "idolization of hate figures", misinformation likely to contribute to imminent violence, doxxing, account hijacking, spam, listed fetish categories, bestiality, graphic violence and threats.

The 2257 statement asserts that the service is exempt from US record-keeping requirements because "our website does not depict actual human beings engaged in real sexually explicit conduct", all content "is exclusively generated by artificial intelligence", users cannot upload images of real people, and "The content created from this website contains only text generated based on AI and does not contain images or videos" ([2257 statement](https://crushon.ai/2257); Last Updated 2025-02-08).

## Engineering

### Technology stack and platforms

No stack page is published. Items below are confirmed from the served application and its network requests, inspected in a browser on 2026-08-14.

- **Front end:** Next.js App Router — chunk paths under `_next/static/chunks/app/[locale]/(dashboard)/…` indicate a locale-segmented App Router build; static assets are served from `static.crushon.ai` and `cdn.crushon.ai`.
- **Edge and bot protection:** the whole site sits behind Cloudflare with an interactive challenge — every automated fetch of `crushon.ai` returned HTTP 403 with `cf-mitigated: challenge` on 2026-08-14, including `robots.txt`-permitted paths; `static.cloudflareinsights.com` is loaded on the page.
- **Error tracking:** self-hosted Sentry at `sentry.crushon.ai`, receiving envelopes from `sentry.javascript.nextjs/7.120.4`.
- **Feature flags and experiments:** self-hosted GrowthBook at `growthbook-api.crushon.ai`, fetching two SDK feature payloads on page load; the analytics payload carries experiment keys such as `banner_download_experiment`, `search_overlay_transition` and `abtest_world_card_home_transition`.
- **Product analytics:** a self-hosted SensorsData endpoint at `sensor.crushon.ai`, loading `sensors/sensorsdata.min.js` (SDK version 1.26.5) and posting events to `sensor.crushon.ai/sensors/ue`, alongside Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.
- **Authentication:** Google Identity Services (`accounts.google.com`); the Privacy Policy adds that Personal Information is received "from Apple, Google, Discord and Line when you use such third-party platforms to sign on".
- **Affiliate tracking:** Rewardful (`r.wdfl.co`).
- **Payments:** Basis Theory for card tokenization, with "Other Payments" offered alongside "Pay with Credit / Debit Card" on the pricing page.
- **Model layer:** the product exposes "Free", "Pro" and "Ultra" model tiers and 8K/16K/24K memory tiers, but no page or policy names a model, provider, or hosting region.
- **No public code:** no GitHub organisation under `crushon` or `crushonai`, and no npm package matching "crushon" (API checks 2026-08-14).

### Data handling as documented

The Privacy Policy states that chat content is used for model training in aggregate: "For all contents generated from the chat, we will only use such contents in a general way for training our models and we will not link chat contents to specific users." Elsewhere it states more broadly, "We may use User Content to train AI models, as well as for our Business Purposes", and, for chats specifically, "We may use User Content from character chats to train AI models" ([Privacy Policy](https://crushon.ai/privacy-policy); Last modified 2025-03-06). No opt-out from training is described.

Retention is open-ended: "We retain Personal Information for so long as it is reasonably necessary to achieve the relevant purposes described in this Policy, or for so long as is required by law", with no stated period ([Privacy Policy](https://crushon.ai/privacy-policy)). At the product level, free-tier chat history is deleted "after 7 days of inactivity" while paid history is "kept during the Membership Period" ([pricing page](https://crushon.ai/pricing)).

The policy names Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, describes data received from Apple, Google, Discord and Line sign-on, and states GDPR and UK data-protection law among the applicable laws; it names no cloud provider, model provider or other subprocessor. It sets the age floor at 18 and says the company "does not knowingly collect any Personal Information about or market to children, minors, or anyone under the age of 18" ([Privacy Policy](https://crushon.ai/privacy-policy)).

The Terms grant the company broad rights over submitted content and include a class-action waiver, a subpoena-cost pass-through ("If Crushon has to provide information in response to a subpoena related to Your account, then we may charge You for our costs"), and export-control compliance obligations ([Terms of Use](https://crushon.ai/terms-of-service)).
