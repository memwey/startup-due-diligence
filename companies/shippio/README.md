## TL;DR

Shippio (株式会社Shippio) is a Tokyo-based company founded in June 2016, originally as サークルイン株式会社 (Circle-in Corp.). It operates **Shippio Platform**, a set of cloud services for international trade and freight — vessel tracking, trade document handling, quotation and ordering, cost analysis — and is simultaneously a licensed freight forwarder and, through its subsidiary 協和海運, a customs broker.

- 1,500+ companies use the platform ([service site](https://service.shippio.io/); Undated; accessed 2026-07-29). Since the 2022 Series B, the company reports net revenue ~20x, containers handled ~35x, and platform users ~8x ([2025-10-30](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/)).
- Cumulative funding approximately ¥7B, most recently a Series C of ¥3.24B (¥1.87B equity + ¥1.37B debt) closed [2025-10-30](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/), led by DNX Ventures.
- Headcount is stated as "over 100" by the CEO ([2026-01-05](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/shippio-2026/)); the company says it aims to reach 300 within three years ([2025-10-30](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/)). Undated third-party profiles are not treated as current point estimates.
- Backend is Ruby on Rails and Go on AWS, frontend React/TypeScript over GraphQL (Apollo Federation) ([TokyoDev profile](https://www.tokyodev.com/companies/shippio), [Back-End Engineer posting](https://herp.careers/v1/shippioinc/tLtoq_zXXM0Z); Undated; accessed 2026-07-29). The engineering team communicates mainly in English; the Back-End Engineer posting requires English and treats Japanese as welcome but not mandatory ([recruit FAQ](https://recruit.shippio.io/faq), [posting](https://herp.careers/v1/shippioinc/tLtoq_zXXM0Z); Undated; accessed 2026-07-29).

### Market context as stated by the company

From the [Series C release, 2025-10-30](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/):

- Customs clearance permits (通関許可件数) increased eightfold between 2016 and 2024, which the company attributes to growth in e-commerce and import/export volumes.
- The company describes trade operations as still analogue and dependent on individual staff (アナログかつ属人的).
- It names US tariffs and the situation in the Middle East as geopolitical risks increasing supply-chain uncertainty.
- Stated target: handle 30% of Japan-origin/destination cargo — 5.4 million TEU annually — on Shippio Platform by 2030.

Feature areas cited on the product pages (Undated; accessed 2026-07-29): automatic vessel tracking with delay alerts; chat and file sharing scoped per shipment; AI-OCR on B/L, invoice and packing list, stated at "97% or higher" reading accuracy; AI invoice-to-purchase-order matching, stated to cut work time by up to 80%; lead-time analytics; quotation and cost simulation. The Forwarding page states 120+ domestic partners and an overseas network across 35+ countries.

### Commercialization

Sold as SaaS alongside the company's own forwarding and customs operations, so revenue mixes subscription and transport/clearance work. No comprehensive public price list was found.

- **Shippio Works** publishes a price on its [product page](https://service.shippio.io/works): ¥80,000/month for up to 50 users, with unlimited external guest invitations and unlimited API connections.
- **Shippio Forwarding**'s [page](https://service.shippio.io/forwarding) states the cloud platform is free when the customer uses Shippio's forwarding services.
- **Shippio Cargo** and **Shippio Clear** pricing is not published.
- **Shippio API** was made free to platform customers on [2025-07-23](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/apifree202507/).

### Stated plans

From the [Series C release (2025-10-30)](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/), funds are allocated to: (1) product development, with the internal logistics operations team and the group customs broker feeding the development team; (2) AI, via a newly established **AI Advanced Lab**, first applied to the company's own logistics operations before being reflected into products; (3) further M&A; (4) organization building toward 300 people in three years, across sales, CS, business development, PdM, product design, engineering, HR and corporate. The release states approximately 20 positions were open at the time.

Longer-term target: continuous 2x growth in net revenue and 30% of Japan-origin/destination cargo (5.4 million TEU/year) on the platform by 2030.

## Engineering

### Technology stack and platforms

Inferred from job postings, the tech blog index, and the TokyoDev profile; labelled as inferred.

- **Backend:** Ruby on Rails / Ruby, and Go. The [TokyoDev profile](https://www.tokyodev.com/companies/shippio) states "a Ruby on Rails and Go Back-End, deployed on AWS." The [Back-End Engineer posting](https://herp.careers/v1/shippioinc/tLtoq_zXXM0Z) lists Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL, Redis, REST API, GraphQL.

**Architecture history.** A [Speaker Deck presentation dated 2022-12-01](https://speakerdeck.com/shippio/voyage-for-the-future-soa-approach-powered-by-apollofederation) describes a migration from a monolithic Rails application to a service-oriented architecture unified behind Apollo Federation, with new services in Go, a multi-account AWS setup, and a move from Terraform to CDK. The project ran September 2021 to August 2022; the deck states the engineering team was around 8 people at the time, targeting roughly 20 after the Series B. A [Japanese summary of the same migration](https://note.com/shippio/n/nc56f93ab661b) is on the company's note.

The company runs an English-language [Tech Blog on Medium](https://techblog.shippio.io/), with sections for Frontend, Backend, Infrastructure, QA and Organization and an [index of past posts on note](https://note.com/shippio/n/n36094bb0a387). ### Technical background sought

The Back-End Engineer posting prefers cloud-infrastructure experience on AWS or GCP and experience translating business requirements into architecture. The QA posting prefers testing experience on web ERP products; its responsibilities cover test planning, automation and quality improvement for the platform. Logistics background is preferred rather than required. No posting reviewed asks for blockchain, crawling or real-time trading experience.

### Industry domain

International freight forwarding and customs clearance — bills of lading, commercial invoices, packing lists, Incoterms, HS codes and origin rules, FCL/LCL ocean freight and air freight, carrier schedules and vessel movements, and the Japanese government systems NACCS and Cyber Port. The company holds forwarding licences itself and owns a customs broker, and the [Series C release](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/) describes the pairing of an in-house logistics operations team and group customs company with the development team as its distinguishing characteristic.

On whether domain knowledge is a prerequisite, the [recruit FAQ](https://recruit.shippio.io/faq) states that many members had not worked in trade or international logistics before, that industry knowledge can be picked up after joining, and that about 50% of employees became interested in the trade industry only after starting the selection process. The QA Engineer posting lists a logistics background as preferred, not required.

### Other

- The business is not pure SaaS: the company holds first- and second-class freight forwarding licences and an air cargo licence, runs its own forwarding operations, owns a customs broker outright, and became a registered insurance agency in December 2025. The [Series C release](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/series-c/) names further M&A as a use of funds.
- The 協和海運 acquisition (2022-09-27, 100% of shares; the target was founded 1960 and based in Yokohama) has been the subject of several public retrospectives by the company and its investors: [Coral Capital (JA)](https://coralcap.co/2024/04/shippio-kyowakaiun/), [note (JA)](https://note.com/shippio/n/n733feb7e39c6), [MAcloud journal (JA)](https://journal.macloud.jp/posts/article_0063).
- Product naming changed on [2025-03-12](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/newmv/), when "Any Cargo" became "Shippio Cargo" and the Shippio Platform umbrella was introduced alongside a new mission (産業の転換点をつくる) and vision (国際物流を、アドバンストに). Releases from 2024 and early 2025 still use the old names.
- Of the 20 most recent items on the company's [press index](https://www.shippio.io/news/press-release/) as of 2026-07-29, 10 are trade-show exhibition announcements.
- The API reference is published openly on [Stoplight](https://shippio.stoplight.io/docs/shippio-api/753cf96a69c40-shippio-api), but the product pages themselves do not link to it; the Shippio API [product page](https://service.shippio.io/shippio-api/) directs readers to a contact form instead.
- The company's own press index lags PR TIMES by a few hours to a few days for the newest items; both were checked for this page.
