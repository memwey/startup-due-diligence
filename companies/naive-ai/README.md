## TL;DR

`naive.ai` is, as of 2026-08-18, a single static page: the words "100 × intelligence for the pioneers", a "Join us" link to `hr@naive.ai`, and nothing else. It is served from GitHub Pages by the organisation `naiveai-team`, which was created on 2026-02-24; its only repository, `landing-page`, was created and last pushed on 2026-03-17 ([naive.ai](https://naive.ai/); [GitHub API](https://api.github.com/orgs/naiveai-team); accessed 2026-08-18). No product, pricing, team, careers, blog or documentation page exists, and no legal entity is named anywhere on the site.

- The company is publicly associated with **Jifeng Dai (代季峰)**, a tenured associate professor in Tsinghua University's Department of Electronic Engineering, but that association comes from third-party reports only: neither [his personal site](https://jifengdai.org/) nor [his Tsinghua faculty page](https://web.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/daijifeng/zh_CN/index.htm) mentions Naive.ai in any form (both accessed 2026-08-18).
- A widely-copied Chinese report says Naive.ai raised "about US$300M" at "about US$800M" valuation from "first-tier funds and tech giants" whose names are "not disclosed". Its provenance is thin: the version reviewed is dated 2026-04-26, attributes the claim only to "据多家媒体报道" ("according to multiple media reports") without naming one, and is itself a republication of a WeChat account post ([AITNT](https://www.aitntnews.com/newDetail.html?newId=24460)). No wire, no Chinese tech outlet of record and no company statement carrying this round was found — see `Funding`.
- Dai's own site does document the immediately preceding chapter: "2025.5 - 2026.1 | Leader of MiroMind, an AGI lab founded by Shanda. Unfortunately, MiroMind stopped operation in China due to geo-political issues in Jan 2026." ([jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/); accessed 2026-08-18). The circumstances of that departure are publicly disputed between Dai and Shanda — see `Notes`.
- There is a separate, unrelated company called **Naïve** (YC Spring 2025, San Francisco, `usenaive.ai`, "Autonomous Company Infrastructure", founders Sean Dorje and Dennis Zax) which search engines and databases frequently return for the same query ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/naive); accessed 2026-08-18) — see `Identity and legal entities`.

## Product

Nothing is published. The entire served page consists of the site name, the line "100 × intelligence for the pioneers", and a "Join us" mailto link; there is no product description, screenshot, waitlist, documentation, pricing or demo ([naive.ai](https://naive.ai/); accessed 2026-08-18).

The only stated product direction found anywhere is in the third-party report described under `Funding`, which says the company works on "开源模型的后训练和 AI Agent 方向" (post-training of open-source models and AI agents) with a core team drawn from former MiroMind members ([AITNT](https://www.aitntnews.com/newDetail.html?newId=24460); Published 2026-04-26). That direction is consistent with, but not confirmed by, Dai's own stated research focus of "agentic AI and continual learning" ([jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/); accessed 2026-08-18). No first-party source states what Naive.ai is building.

## Engineering

### Technology stack and platforms

There is essentially no engineering surface to observe. Everything below was confirmed on 2026-08-18.

- **The site is a static page on GitHub Pages.** `naive.ai` resolves to `naiveai-team.github.io` and to the GitHub Pages address range; responses carry `server: GitHub.com` and a `last-modified` of 2026-03-17. Cloudflare name servers are configured at the registrar, but the origin is GitHub Pages.
- **The repository contains a landing page and nothing else** — `naiveai-team/landing-page`, language CSS, created 2026-03-17, seven commits the same day (`init landing page`, three `Create CNAME`/`Delete CNAME` pairs, `fix gpt typo`), no releases, no stars.
- **The page loads no analytics, no error tracking and no third-party scripts**; its only external requests are to Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) for the Cormorant Garamond, Instrument Serif and Space Grotesk families.
- **No application surface exists.** `/about`, `/team`, `/careers`, `/jobs`, `/blog` and `/research` return HTTP 404, and `app.naive.ai`, `api.naive.ai` and `docs.naive.ai` do not resolve.
- **No other public code.** The organisation has one repository and no public members; no npm or PyPI package under the name was found.

### Technical background sought

Nothing is published by the company. The only hiring signal on `naive.ai` is the `hr@naive.ai` mailto link behind "Join us", with no roles, locations, requirements or compensation stated. The lab hiring notice on Dai's personal site is for Tsinghua University positions — internships, Ph.D. and postdoctoral places in agentic AI and continual learning — not for the company ([jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/); accessed 2026-08-18).

### Industry domain

The public evidence supports only what Dai's own research record describes: multi-modal foundation models, visual perception, autonomous-driving perception and, currently, "agentic AI and continual learning" ([jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/), [Tsinghua faculty page](https://web.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/daijifeng/zh_CN/index.htm)). The reported company direction — post-training of open-source models and agents — rests on the unverified report in `Funding`.

### Inconsistencies across sources

- **The company is claimed for a person whose own pages do not mention it.** Third-party reports name Jifeng Dai as Naive.ai's founder, while [jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/) — which is otherwise maintained in detail, listing roles up to "2025.5 - 2026.1" — and his [Tsinghua faculty page](https://web.ee.tsinghua.edu.cn/daijifeng/zh_CN/index.htm) contain no reference to it (both accessed 2026-08-18).
- **Funding: asserted versus untraceable.** The "~US$300M / ~US$800M" figures are stated as fact by [AITNT](https://www.aitntnews.com/newDetail.html?newId=24460) and as rumour ("传") by [mirror sites](https://www.jhth.cn/live/56926.html); the former cites only "多家媒体报道" and names none. No corroborating primary source was found.
- **MiroMind tenure dates.** Dai's own site gives "2025.5 - 2026.1" ([jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/)); Chinese self-media accounts give a March 2025 start and an 18 January 2026 resignation from a "技术顾问" (technical adviser) role ([Sina 财经头条 repost](https://t.cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/2118746300/7e4980bc02001m9jg)). The two descriptions of the role and its dates do not match.
- **Name collision with a separate company.** Databases and search engines return the YC Spring 2025 company Naïve (`usenaive.ai`, San Francisco) for the same queries; it is a different company with different founders ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/naive)).

### Other

- **The public footprint is three months old and static.** The GitHub organisation was created 2026-02-24, the page was pushed 2026-03-17, and the served page has not changed since — its `last-modified` header still reads 2026-03-17 when fetched on 2026-08-18.
- **The circumstances of Dai's departure from MiroMind are publicly disputed, and both sides' positions are recorded here without adjudication.** Dai's own site attributes MiroMind's China shutdown to "geo-political issues" in January 2026 ([jifengdai.org](https://jifengdai.org/)). Reporting attributed to the Washington Post, carried in April 2026 by Bloomberg-syndicated and other outlets, states that Dai told the Post he left because the company required AI researchers to relocate abroad, and that MiroMind moved its staff out of China after Beijing cautioned against transferring core talent and results overseas ([Seoul Economic Daily summary of the Washington Post reporting, 2026-04-22](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/04/22/ai-startup-miromind-flees-china-amid-beijings-tightening); [Crain Currency, Bloomberg syndication](https://www.craincurrency.com/global/chinese-billionaire-chen-tianqiao-overhauls-ai-startup-after-warning-manus)). Chinese self-media accounts describe a subsequent Shanda internal notice disputing that characterisation ([Sina 财经头条 repost](https://t.cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/2118746300/7e4980bc02001m9jg)). The primary Washington Post article and the Shanda notice were not read directly; the syndicated pages returned HTTP 403 to automated access on 2026-08-18. This page records the existence and attribution of these accounts only.
- **MiroMind itself continues to operate a public site** titled "MiroMind | General Purpose Solver" ([miromind.ai](https://www.miromind.ai/); accessed 2026-08-18).
- **The landing page's copy is the only statement of intent the company has made:** "100 × intelligence for the pioneers" ([naive.ai](https://naive.ai/)).
