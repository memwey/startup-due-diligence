## TL;DR

NETSTARS (株式会社ネットスターズ) is a Tokyo-based payment gateway company founded on 2009-02-12 and listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth Market since 2023-09-26 (code 5590). Its main product, **StarPay**, lets a merchant contract for and operate 40+ QR code payment brands plus credit cards and e-money through a single application, settlement, and management flow; a second line, **StarPay-DX**, sells self-checkout, mobile ordering, reservation and mini-app products into the same merchant base.

- FY2025 (year to 2025-12) payment volume (GPV) was **¥2,122.8bn**, up 33.2% year on year; revenue **¥4,788m** (+22.7%) and operating profit **¥293m** — the first full-year operating profit since listing ([FY2025 results release](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/tdnet/2757309/00.pdf), [FY2025 annual securities report p.21](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)).
- Roughly **700,000 accounts** (stores, vending machines, ticket machines) domestically as of 2025-12-31, from ~15,000 directly contracted companies ([annual securities report p.6](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf), [infographics](https://www.netstars.co.jp/infographics/) — Undated; accessed 2026-07-29).
- Headcount **221 consolidated** at 2025-12-31, of which 146 at the Tokyo parent and 70 at the Dalian development subsidiary. Parent-company average age 38.2, average tenure 3.8 years, average annual pay **¥6.93m** ([annual securities report pp.10, 23](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)).
- Engineering is described by the company as cloud-native with container technology on AWS (and, per its own slides, Google); it reports **99.999%** payment success rate and 40 ms per transaction for calendar 2025, and states R&D activity as "none" in its filing ([Q1 FY2026 presentation](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/tdnet/2811540/00.pdf), [annual securities report p.22](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)).

## Product

### StarPay — multi-cashless payment gateway

A merchant signs once with NETSTARS and gains QR code payments, credit cards, common point programmes, e-money and (from 2026) stablecoins, with unified onboarding, screening, settlement and reporting through the **StarPay-Works** management site ([StarPay site](https://starpay.netstars.co.jp/)). Deployment forms are a dedicated NETSTARS terminal, iOS/Android apps, or API integration into an existing POS, vending machine, ticket machine, locker or online checkout ([StarPay FAQ](https://starpay.netstars.co.jp/faq/)). The marketing site states a **99.9% uptime guarantee**, a 24/7/365 support centre, PCI DSS certification, and a minimum one-week onboarding time.

Brand coverage as of the [March 2026 growth-plan deck](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/tdnet/2783892/00.pdf): **40+ QR brands, 6 credit card brands, 7 e-money brands**, with "10 or more" further payment brands planned for FY2026. The [infographics page](https://www.netstars.co.jp/infographics/) adds that the foreign QR services usable in Japan through StarPay originate from **13 countries/regions** (data as of 2025-12).

### Commercialization

Described in the [annual securities report p.7](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf):

- **Payment**: NETSTARS receives a merchant fee proportional to payment value; its revenue is that fee net of the payment provider's fee. For directly contracted merchants it receives the settlement from each payment brand, deducts its fee, and remits to the merchant the following month — which is why funds held on behalf of merchants (預り金, ¥30,131m at 2025-12-31) dominate the balance sheet. For OEM partners it takes a fee on total payment value. Where a referral agent (取次店) brought the merchant, NETSTARS pays that agent a volume-based fee, recorded as a cost.
- **DX products**: an initial development/onboarding fee, a monthly usage and maintenance fee that varies by function, and payment fees on volume flowing through the DX product.
- **Terminals** are sold to merchants that want them; terminal sales carry lower gross margin than payment fees and moved Q4 FY2025 gross margin down ([FY2025 results Q&A — 2026-02-20](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/ir_material_for_fiscal_ym/199265/00.pdf)).
- **Stablecoin Pay** publishes a **0.98%** transaction fee ([2026-07-13 release](https://www.netstars.co.jp/news/9450/)).

No general public price list for StarPay was found; the site routes to an application form or a consultation form.

### Stated plans

The company published a "2030 target state" in its [March 2026 investor deck](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/ir_material_for_fiscal_ym1/200899/00.pdf) and [growth-plan disclosure](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/tdnet/2783892/00.pdf):

- GPV **over ¥6tn**, payment-related revenue **over ¥10bn**, total revenue **over ¥12bn** by FY2030, from the current business organically. The disclosure labels this a target GPV CAGR of **+25.0%** for 2023–2030, against a "progress CAGR" of +24.6% for 2023–2026.
- Gross margin maintained above **70%**; operating and ordinary margin above **25%** (FY2025 actual operating margin 6.1%).
- Overseas: add regions and products beyond the current **Qatar, Cambodia and Mongolia**.
- Further use of cloud computing, Web3.0 and AI to cut server and development cost, and to build vertical-specific payment/remittance/DX products; M&A named as a route into new domains.
- FY2026 guidance: revenue ¥5,760m (+20.3%), operating profit ¥500m (+70.8%), ordinary profit ¥707m (+59.7%), net income ¥493m (+1.7%), GPV ¥2,547.4bn (+20.0%) ([FY2025 results release](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/tdnet/2757309/00.pdf)).
- No dividend has ever been paid, and none is planned for FY2026 ([annual securities report p.17](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)).

### Industry domain

Japanese retail payments regulation and the mechanics of multi-party settlement. From the filings:

- **Regulatory surface**: the Installment Sales Act (割賦販売法) and industry guidelines govern merchant management and security; NETSTARS holds a funds transfer service registration and runs cross-border JPQR Global, and names regulatory change as a risk (likelihood medium, impact small) ([annual securities report p.15](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)). PCI DSS 4.0.1 full compliance is maintained ([company page](https://www.netstars.co.jp/about)).
- **Settlement mechanics**: the four-step flow of payment information and funds between consumer, merchant, QR provider and NETSTARS, and the resulting 預り金 liability, are set out in the [annual securities report p.7](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf); the balance was ¥30,131m at FY2025 end.
- **Counterparty contracts**: the disclosed framework agreements are with Tencent (WeChat Pay, from 2015-04-27, 1-year auto-renewal), Alipay Singapore (2021-07-30 to 2024-07-29, 3-year auto-renewal), NTT Docomo d払い (from 2018-05-22, terminable on notice), PayPay (from 2018-10-01, 1-year auto-renewal) and KDDI (from 2019-03-28, 1-year auto-renewal) ([p.22](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)).
- **Geopolitical exposure**: the filing names the possibility that Chinese-origin QR brands (Alipay, WeChat Pay) become unavailable through international disputes as a business risk ([p.14](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/yuho_pdf/S100XTGZ/00.pdf)). Overseas QR (inbound) was 7.3% of FY2025 GPV; the company estimates the Chinese-inbound decline cost about −1.2% of GPV and about −2% of revenue on an annualised basis ([March 2026 deck](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/ir_material_for_fiscal_ym1/200899/00.pdf), [FY2025 Q&A](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/5590/ir_material_for_fiscal_ym/199265/00.pdf)).
- **Blockchain**: the 2026 product direction requires working across chains (Solana, Polygon, Aptos, Canton), wallets (MetaMask, Bitget Wallet, imToken) and stablecoins (USDC, USDT, JPYC, and Startale's JPYSC/USDSC), plus Circle's Gateway cross-chain infrastructure ([2026-07-13 release](https://www.netstars.co.jp/news/9450/), [2026-04-13 release](https://www.netstars.co.jp/news/9148/)).

No source reviewed states whether engineers are expected to acquire this domain knowledge, or how.
